# Dawn of Sorrow 12-bit release

This source adds MWK revision 0F. Nitro IMA ADPCM is retained in the KSP and
decoded at track load time to packed 12-bit YMF278B PCM. The resulting full
A Fleeting Respite bank fits a 640 KiB MoonSound RAM expansion. Revision 0E
continues to decode to full 16-bit PCM unchanged.
